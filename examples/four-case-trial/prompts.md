Attach short/before.docx and short/before.pdf.

You may reorganize and tighten the wording without changing meaning. Choose a fitting visual direction and make the sessions, preparation, arrival and cancellation information easy to find. Return a one-page A4 editable DOCX and a matching PDF exported from that DOCX. Use only free tools. Do not add rules, personal information, unsupported claims or a new contact address. Inspect the final page before delivery. Keep design rationale and production notes outside the document.
