# Workshop handout source notes

Fictional institution: Morrow Museum.
Audience: first-time visitors choosing and attending a postcard-printing workshop.
Purpose: help visitors choose a session, prepare, check in, and know what to do if they cannot attend.

- Morning session: 10:00–11:15, 12 places.
- Afternoon session: 14:00–15:15, 12 places.
- Both sessions have the same activity: make two single-color postcards.
- Participation free. Book at museum desk. One booking reserves one place. No experience needed.
- Arrive ten minutes before the session starts; check in at museum desk in entrance hall.
- Room: Studio 2, ground floor. Ask the desk for help finding it.
- Wear clothes that may get ink on them.
- Museum provides paper, water-based ink, rollers, printing blocks and aprons. Participants bring no materials.
- Activity sequence: facilitator demonstrates; participant chooses a supplied block, rolls on ink, prints two postcards.
- Prints stay on drying rack until the end of the session. Participants take both cards home afterward.
- If unable to attend, cancel at museum desk before the session starts so another visitor can use the place.
- Questions about the activity or getting to the studio go to the museum desk.
- No further rules, age restriction, access guarantee, contact address or health claim is supplied.
