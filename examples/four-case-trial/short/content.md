# Print a postcard

Morrow Museum runs a fictional introductory postcard-printing workshop for first-time visitors. This handout helps visitors choose a session, prepare, and find their place on arrival.

## Sessions

| Session | Time | Places | What participants make |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | 10:00–11:15 | 12 | Two single-color postcards |
| Afternoon | 14:00–15:15 | 12 | Two single-color postcards |

Both sessions follow the same activity. Participation is free. Booking is required at the museum desk. One booking reserves one place. No experience is needed.

## Before the session

Arrive ten minutes before the session starts and check in at the museum desk. Wear clothes that can get ink on them. The museum supplies paper, water-based ink, rollers, printing blocks, and aprons. Participants do not need to bring materials.

## During the session

The facilitator demonstrates the process. Participants choose a supplied printing block, roll on ink, and print two postcards. Prints stay on the drying rack until the end of the session. Participants take both postcards home after the session.

## Finding the room

Check in at the desk in the entrance hall. The workshop takes place in Studio 2 on the ground floor. Tell the desk if you need help finding the room.

## If plans change

If you cannot attend, cancel at the museum desk before the session starts so the place can be offered to another visitor.

## Questions

Ask the museum desk about the activity or getting to the studio.
